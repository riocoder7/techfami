Do I really not pay anything first?

Correct. You tell us your idea, our team understands it and creates a demo for you to see. The demo is free. We only continue after you confirm.

What if I do not like the demo?

Then nothing happens. You owe us nothing for the demo and there is no bill.

What can you build?

Websites, mobile apps for Android and iOS, AI chatbots and generative AI features, custom AI development, UI/UX design and SaaS products. We also maintain software you already have and add new features to existing websites, web apps and mobile apps. And we build and run our own product, Techfami ERP.

How much does it cost?

The Starter website plan starts at ₹19,999 (about $250). Pro Business is ₹39,999 ($450) for a dynamic website or a mobile app, and Enterprise starts at ₹49,999 ($700). You always get a clear quote before we build.

How long does a project take?

A Starter one-page website is delivered in 15 days. Larger projects depend on what you need, and we give you the timeline together with your quote.

Do you support the project after launch?

Yes. The Starter plan and the Pro Business app option include 12 months of free support, and Enterprise includes ongoing maintenance and upgrades.
