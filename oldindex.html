What is Techfami ERP?

Techfami ERP is a powerful, cloud-based Enterprise Resource Planning platform designed to help businesses manage their day-to-day operations from one centralized system.

Built for garment, textile, manufacturing, trading, and other growing businesses, Techfami ERP brings essential operations such as billing, sales, purchases, inventory, production, payments, reporting, and analytics together in one easy-to-use platform.

It runs entirely in a web browser, so there is no complicated installation or infrastructure required. Simply sign up, log in, and manage your business from anywhere, on any device.

Manufacturers

Manufacturing businesses that need to manage production, raw materials, inventory, sales, purchases, billing, and daily operations from one system.

Garment & Textile Businesses

Garment and textile businesses can manage production processes, stock, client orders, challans, deliveries, GST invoices, and business records in one centralized platform.

Traders & Distributors

Trading and distribution businesses can manage customers, suppliers, sales, purchases, inventory, payments, outstanding balances, and business performance with ease.

Core Modules — Everything You Need

GST Billing & Invoice

Generate professional GST-compliant invoices and bills instantly. Supports custom product fields like MRP, color, size, and fabric type. Export as PDF in one click. Manage financial year-wise billing records with full history.

Challan Management

Create and track delivery challans with LR number, transport details, and delivery location. Update status in real time — Pending, Delivered, or Returned. View challan analytics with date-range filtering and export full reports to Excel.

Raw Stock & Inventory

Track raw fabric and material stock month by month across financial years. Manage products by color, category, and custom fields. Search and filter inventory instantly, and export full stock reports to Excel for audits or review.

Advance Payment Tracking

Record advance payments received from clients with multiple payment modes. Automatically tracks status — Pending, Fully Adjusted, or Extra. Generates advance receipt PDFs. Never lose track of how much a client owes or has paid in advance.

Analytics Dashboard

A powerful business intelligence dashboard with sales trend charts, product performance reports, challan analytics, MRP value tracking, and monthly comparisons. Filter by date range and export all data to Excel instantly.

Multi-user Role Access

Invite your staff with role-based permissions — control who can view, create, edit, or delete each module. Admin sees everything; staff only sees what they need. Keeps your business data secure and organized.
