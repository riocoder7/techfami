Techfami ERP

A cloud-based Enterprise Resource Planning platform that runs in a web browser. Sign up, log in and manage your business from anywhere, on any device.

Visit live platform Create free account

Techfami ERP brings billing, sales, purchases, inventory, production, payments, reporting and analytics into one easy-to-use platform, built for garment, textile, manufacturing, trading and other growing businesses.

Manufacturers

Production, raw materials, inventory, sales, purchases and billing from one system.

Garment and textile

Stock, client orders, challans, deliveries and GST invoices in one centralised place.

Traders and distributors

Customers, suppliers, sales, purchases, payments and outstanding balances with ease.

Every module your workflow needs.

From product development and sampling to production, inventory, billing, payments, employees and reporting.

Production

  • Product managementCreate products with flexible, dynamic information instead of fixed fields.
  • Dynamic fields and columnsAdd your own fields across products, challans, invoices and other records.
  • Sampling managementTrack samples from creation to completion, with categories, status, dates and quantities.
  • Production managementCreate production orders, assign products and quantities, and follow completed versus remaining work.
  • Process trackingDefine steps such as knitting, dyeing, cutting, stitching and finishing, and track quantities per process.
  • Employee and production paymentsCalculate worker payments from completed quantities or processes and keep payment records.

Billing and sales

  • GST billing and invoicesGST-compliant invoices with custom columns, one-click PDF and financial-year records.
  • Delivery challan and LRChallans with LR number, transport details and delivery location. Status tracking, PDF and Excel reports.
  • Sales and purchaseManage customers, suppliers, sales and purchases with connected records.
  • Payments and outstandingRecord advances and payments, track adjustments and outstanding balances, download PDF receipts.

Stock and insight

  • Inventory and stockRaw materials and finished goods by color, category and quantity, with opening balances, history, search and filters.
  • Analytics and reportsDashboards, sales trends, production and payment reports with date-range filters and Excel export.
  • Multi-user role accessRole-based permissions decide who can view, create, edit or delete in each module.
